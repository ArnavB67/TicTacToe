This is TicTacToe but with a twist
the twist is that at a time only 3 of X or 3 of O can remain on board
while placing the 4th one the oldes move gets removed,this leads to game not ending on a draw and brings more aspects to the plain old tic tac toe
left click to place your move
the game operates on turn to turn basis on the same computer
